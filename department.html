DEPARTMENT OF COMPUTER APPLICATIONS

Here you can know about the Department of Computer Application

The Department of Computer Applications is committed to excellence in education, research, and innovation. We offer industry-oriented programmes that equip students with technical expertise, problem-solving abilities, and leadership skills to thrive in the dynamic digital world. Our programmes MCA and BCA (Hons.) are designed to build strong foundations in computing, analytics, and business applications while encouraging creativity, collaboration, and ethical values.

Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA Hons.)

Shape the Future with Technology

The Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA Hons.) at MCET is a four-year undergraduate programme affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU), Kerala. Designed to meet the evolving demands of the digital era, the programme equips students with a strong foundation in computer science, software development, and emerging technologies while nurturing innovation, analytical thinking, and problem-solving skills.

The curriculum integrates academic excellence with experiential learning, enabling students to master programming, software engineering, data management, cloud technologies, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and modern computing practices. Through a blend of classroom instruction, laboratory sessions, industry internships, research activities, and live projects, students graduate as industry-ready professionals capable of addressing real-world technological challenges.

Master of Computer Applications (MCA)

Innovate. Develop. Lead the Digital Future.

The Master of Computer Applications (MCA) at Mohandas College of Engineering and Technology (MCET) is a two-year postgraduate programme affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU) , Kerala. The programme is designed to develop highly skilled computing professionals capable of addressing the evolving challenges of the global technology ecosystem.

With a strong emphasis on academic excellence, innovation, and industry relevance, the programme provides an advanced understanding of computer science, software engineering, intelligent computing , cloud technologies, data analytics, and secure computing. Students are equipped with the knowledge and practical competencies required to design, develop, deploy, and manage modern software systems and digital solutions.
